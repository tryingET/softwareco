# Authority review — CTO Agent and template activation

## Verdict

`revise_rfc`

## Blocking findings

1. The Org Owner activation must be conditional on an accepted AK decision and identify appointee, domain, reserved decisions, review date, revocation, evidence, and strategic-frame linkage.
2. The CTO delegation must distinguish may-decide from may-execute, name consultation/escalation and evidence obligations, and explicitly reserve terminal, privacy, consent, ethics, licensing, authority-boundary, and cross-domain decisions.
3. The RFC must identify the exact Decision 62 ordering/propagation clauses it overrides and state whether new L2 generation is frozen before canary acceptance.
4. The lifecycle must separate acceptance, plans, L1 mutation, an owner-approved issue-tracker canary exception, outcome evidence/learning, terminal human decision, and later broad propagation.

## Positive findings

The RFC correctly keeps AK authoritative, FCOS coordination-only, and excludes permanent CTO appointment, L0 propagation, external issue mutation, and FCOS authority change.
