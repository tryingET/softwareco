# Problem and intent — recurring Softwareco portfolio CTO

## Trigger

Decision 74 established a constitutional CTO workbench and proved one owner-native portfolio wave. Human terminal receipt `8870` then lawfully ended that finite mandate. The completed canary is valid evidence, but it was mistakenly treated as completion of the broader operator objective.

The operator corrected the objective on 2026-07-25: Softwareco needs a **recurring operating loop**, not only one successful canary.

## Current facts

The completed Decision 74 slice proved:

- trusted-root `/cto` discovery and fail-closed behavior;
- zero-state sensing without inventing consent;
- direct Product/Project owner acceptance;
- serialized portfolio admission;
- source-owner execution and evidence;
- terminal owner acceptance, WIP release, outcome evidence, and handback.

It did not prove:

- reconstruction from durable state after a fresh-session restart;
- a maintained portfolio thesis after the first outcome;
- a second independent owner outcome;
- re-entry into sensing after a completed and released wave;
- separation between objective completion, proof maturity, epoch handback, and standing-mandate termination.

The current implementation is also bound to Decision `74`, controller task `4182`, fixed timestamps, terminal receipt `8870`, and the first wave. It is an instance verifier, not a recurring operator contract.

## Corrected operator intent

Establish and dogfood a recurring Softwareco CTO operating loop that can:

1. start in a fresh Pi process without inherited conversation state;
2. resolve exact current authority from AK;
3. reconstruct portfolio membership, prior outcomes, WIP, owner posture, and uncertainties from AK and source-owner facts;
4. record a freshness-bounded, proposal-only portfolio thesis in AK evidence;
5. select and execute successive owner-native waves without absorbing owner authority;
6. re-enter after outcome and release to produce the next thesis revision;
7. verify at least one additional independent portfolio outcome beyond the Decision 74 DesignMD canary;
8. remain advisory whenever the standing mandate or current operating epoch is absent, expired, handed back, revoked, terminal, superseded, or ambiguous.

## Completion contract for the current objective

The current objective is complete only when all of the following are observed:

- a successor decision is accepted and a bounded operating epoch is lawfully active;
- a fresh `--no-session` process reconstructs current authority and portfolio state without receiving the earlier thesis or transcript;
- a new AK portfolio-thesis evidence revision cites its durable facts and distinguishes observation, inference, uncertainty, and proposal;
- a non-DesignMD outcome wave obtains direct owner acceptance, is admitted, executes through an exact owner task, passes owner validation, receives terminal acceptance, releases WIP, and records portfolio outcome evidence;
- a later fresh `--no-session` process reconstructs that completed outcome and released capacity, then produces the next proposal-only sensing state;
- the objective closeout does **not** automatically terminalize the standing mandate.

A passing workbench check, one implementation commit, one wave, or one proof record alone is insufficient.

## Authority boundary

Decision 74 and receipt `8870` remain immutable terminal history. A successor requires a new Tier-1 AK decision and new decision-specific receipts, controller identity, task, and evidence. Task `4205` authorizes only governance work in its declared scope; it is not portfolio CTO authority.

AK remains authority for decisions, tasks, receipts, evidence, and direction lineage. Source owners retain task lifecycle and acceptance. FCOS remains owner-controlled Layer-5 coordination only. Pi behavior grants no authority.

## Why this remains Tier 1

The correction changes the lifecycle of the delegated office from one finite canary to a standing mandate with bounded operating epochs, fresh-session continuity, and repeated portfolio execution. It changes authority, controller, evidence, and terminal semantics across owner repositories and therefore requires the full problem → evidence → RFC → review → ADR → execution membrane.
