## Finite experiments and receiver choices

For S7, use the existing agent-host requirements/language/XENO products as proposed
inputs, not accepted implementation truth. Re-read their bytes, task/decision records
and source-owner instructions under a successor scope. Resolve the actual receiver,
ordering/authentication, custody/capacity, failure domain/retention, finite fixture
budget and deployed confinement inputs before running an experiment.

Compare TypeScript/pi-durable and Elixir/raw OTP under identical admitted M0
obligations; no winner or language adoption is established here. Common Lisp is an
operator-suggested option, not a chosen implementation. A pure serializable decision
core, tagged intents and one mission executor are a candidate design, not shipped
behavior. Rust/other frameworks require demonstrated leverage rather than another
unbounded architecture round. The trial must retain timing/resource figures,
actual accepted/rejected work, recovery cuts and reasons that would reverse its
recommendation. The receiver fixes pass/fail thresholds before the trial.

For S8, borrow OptMem chronological recursive compression/recall and Souls
scope/lifetime distinctions as concepts. Separate working context, process state,
actor continuity, source authority and general learning; do not build another
sovereign permission store. Preserve originals and provenance under actual owner
retention/withdrawal terms; a cache reset is not deletion of originals. Use an
uncompressed control, lost-exception/supersession probes and denial canaries before
any memory-fit claim. Provider disclosure admission precedes release.
