## Second-order acceptance questions

A receiver should inspect more than the first successful task:

1. Does Principal initiative reduce reconstruction and complete useful technical
   outcomes, or just increase owner-task/capacity overhead?
2. Are proposed blockers investigated causally and routed correctly, or converted
   into repeated CEO approval questions and ticket proliferation?
3. Does memory preserve exceptions/supersession and actual data-owner rights, or
   reward shorter summaries that become shadow directives?
4. Does source configuration hide volatile decisions, or create an uncontrolled
   override system with competing Git/Dolt/template authority?

These are fitness questions for the actual receiver/owners, not fabricated scores,
statistical evidence or simulated expert judgment.
