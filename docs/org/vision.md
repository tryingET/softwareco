# Softwareco vision

## Three-to-five-year vision

> **AI Society's software layer becomes a coherent local-first environment in which people act primarily through trustworthy steward experiences; governed work moves from intent to outcome with explicit authority, evidence, and recovery; and independently owned systems interoperate without forcing users to understand their internal machinery. Softwareco reliably builds and evolves that environment.**

## The future we are building

### Human-facing capability, not raw machinery

People can initiate, understand, approve, contest, and follow consequential work through clear product and steward experiences. Direct operation of AK, FCOS, Pi internals, deployment substrates, or repository control planes is exceptional rather than the normal human interface.

### Governed agent execution

Agents can perform substantial discovery, engineering, review, operation, and support work, but their authority is explicit and bounded. Material transitions carry source-owner references, guardrails, evidence, and recovery or compensation semantics. Humans retain responsibility for high-impact decisions.

### Local-first AI as dependable infrastructure

Text, speech, image, video, retrieval, and agent capabilities are discoverable and usable through stable local contracts. Models and runtimes can evolve without every product rebuilding lifecycle, resource, privacy, and receipt handling from scratch.

### Products that prove the platform

A small number of products deliver measurable benefit for teaching, learning, households, knowledge work, accessibility, and small institutions. They are not showcases: they have users, owners, support boundaries, outcome evidence, and honest lifecycle decisions.

### A trustworthy engineering factory

Softwareco repeatedly turns validated demand into operated outcomes:

```text
signal
-> discovery
-> finite commitment
-> owner-native delivery
-> safe promotion
-> operation
-> outcome review
-> learning or changed direction
```

Selection, delivery, deployment, operation, and outcome are distinct, traceable facts. Stopping weak work is treated as healthy governance rather than failure.

### Open and sustainable participation

Softwareco is known as a responsible open-source participant and maintainer. It contributes upstream, documents divergence, preserves user freedom where practical, and funds ongoing responsibility through aligned revenue or partnerships rather than surveillance or lock-in.

## Observable success signals

By this vision horizon:

1. **Steward-facing use:** ordinary users can complete consequential workflows without operating raw control-plane tools.
2. **Authority integrity:** no major product relies on a shadow database, undocumented approval path, or prompt-only governance for material actions.
3. **Operational trust:** every supported operated service has an accountable owner, health evidence, incident path, and tested recovery posture appropriate to risk.
4. **Product proof:** at least two materially different human-facing verticals show sustained use and measurable user outcomes.
5. **Platform pull:** shared platform capabilities are adopted because products need them, with owner-repo proof and reduced duplicated implementation.
6. **Local-first resilience:** core workflows remain inspectable and usable without mandatory dependence on one external cloud or vendor.
7. **Learning velocity:** repeated delivery evidence changes engineering standards, templates, product choices, or governance through accepted owner paths.
8. **Sustainability:** mature products or capabilities have an explicit maintenance and economic model consistent with public-benefit commitments.

## Vision boundaries

This vision does not claim that the full steward runtime, runtime cell, governed cognition runtime, or product portfolio already exists. It sets direction. Current capability truth remains with owner repositories and runtime authority surfaces.
