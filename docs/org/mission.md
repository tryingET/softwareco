# Softwareco mission

## Mission statement

> **Design, build, integrate, and operate the software through which AI Society becomes useful in practice—its governed agent systems, local AI capabilities, engineering platforms, evidence paths, runtime infrastructure, and steward-facing products—while preserving human agency and the authority boundaries of the wider society.**

## Proposed strategic posture

Softwareco's intended company posture is **platform first, proved through selected verticals**. As of 2026-07-12 it is narrative direction, not an activated AK strategic frame or a claim that every listed journey is supported.

We will first make the shared foundations coherent and trustworthy:

- AK-backed direction, tasks, decisions, evidence, and lineage;
- Pi-hosted agent execution, delegation, supervision, and operator experience;
- local AI capability discovery, lifecycle, resource, and receipt contracts;
- software intelligence, testing, dependency, quality, and replay systems;
- workstation, provisioning, service, backup, and recovery infrastructure;
- release, promotion, observability, and service-ownership disciplines.

We will prove those foundations through a small number of human-facing products where local-first and governed AI can create meaningful benefit, especially for:

- teaching and learning;
- households and personal knowledge work;
- accessibility and communication;
- small institutions and bounded working groups;
- AI Society's own operators and stewards.

## Operating commitments

### Build products

A Softwareco product should turn validated needs into an accessible, maintainable experience with a named owner, explicit maturity, measurable outcomes, and honest support boundaries. Current support must be proved by its owner repository and operational evidence.

### Build platforms only when products need them

Shared capabilities should remove repeated friction across products. A platform is justified by demonstrated reuse, not architectural elegance alone. Product evidence must shape platform scope.

### Operate what we release

An operated service has an owner, version, health signal, support boundary, incident path, recovery posture, and retirement path. Code completion is not service completion.

### Develop with and for agents

Softwareco uses harnessed agents as engineering collaborators under explicit scope, evidence, review, and human accountability. Individual execution and intelligence capabilities are current only where their owner code, tests, and operator paths prove them.

### Participate responsibly in open source

Softwareco should contribute upstream when alignment exists, carry compatibility evidence for strategic consumed projects, and fork deliberately when local responsibility and divergence are clearer than repeated upstream pressure.

### Compound learning

Delivery evidence should improve tests, contracts, runbooks, templates, tools, and governance. Reusable learning should be promoted through KES and owner surfaces rather than left in sessions or prose; the presence of a KES directory alone is not evidence that promotion occurred.

## Lane responsibilities

| Lane | Mission contribution |
|---|---|
| `owned/` | Directly operated products, platforms, and capability code for which Softwareco accepts product responsibility. |
| `infra/` | Provisioning, runtime, reliability, recovery, and internal operational infrastructure. |
| `contrib/` | Upstream-coupled work, compatibility evidence, and responsible contribution without false ownership claims. |
| `agents/` | Declared target lane for dedicated agent-product archetypes; not materialized at the Softwareco root as of 2026-07-12. The currently present `softwareco-agents/` path is not silently reclassified as this lane. |
| `fork/` | Maintained divergence where upstream scope or roadmap no longer fits and Softwareco accepts the maintenance burden. |

## Intentionally out of scope

Softwareco will not:

- create another canonical society database or universal authority layer;
- treat capability maps, demos, or documentation as production proof;
- optimize delivery throughput without validated demand and outcome feedback;
- run many indefinite product experiments without owners, timeboxes, or stop decisions;
- centralize every product decision at company level;
- replace governance, semantic, procedure, or empirical-analysis owners by convenience;
- deploy consequential systems whose consent, privacy, safety, recovery, or accountability exists only in prompts;
- claim commercial maturity before products have external evidence and sustainable support.

## Mission test

A proposed initiative belongs in Softwareco when it can answer:

1. Whose real problem becomes easier?
2. Which product, platform, service, or engineering capability is responsible?
3. Who is the accountable human owner?
4. What authority boundaries must remain intact?
5. What evidence would justify continued investment?
6. How can the change be contained, reversed, or retired?

If these cannot be answered, the initiative needs discovery—not implementation momentum.
