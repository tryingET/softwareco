# Softwareco values and ethics

> **Company-policy status as of 2026-07-12: proposed, not independently activated.** Existing law, AI Society constitutional constraints, repository safety rules, and valid owner delegations remain binding. This document proposes stricter Softwareco commitments but does not create a new decider or fill the vacant Softwareco Org Owner role.

## Values in practice

### Human agency over automated convenience

Software should expand a person's ability to understand and choose. Consequential actions require an appropriate human path for consent, override, explanation, and appeal. Agents may be powerful delegates; they are not hidden sovereigns.

### Local-first where it protects autonomy

Prefer architectures that keep important data and capability under the user's or operating jurisdiction's control. Cloud services may be used when justified, but mandatory dependency, data extraction, and opaque remote control require explicit benefit and risk review.

### Truthful capability claims

Say what is proposed, implemented, supported, and operated—and keep those states distinct. Higher-level maps and docs may summarize owner truth but may not upgrade it. Uncertainty and missing evidence must be visible.

### Evidence before confidence

Tests, receipts, runtime observations, user evidence, and recovery exercises outrank confidence theater. Evidence supports judgment; it does not eliminate accountability.

### Reversibility and containment

Prefer small, inspectable, reversible changes. High-impact or irreversible transitions require explicit authority, blast-radius control, recovery or compensation, and stronger review.

### Privacy and dignity by design

Collect the minimum necessary data. Treat personal, educational, household, care, and behavioral information as sensitive. Warm interfaces do not excuse opaque manipulation or weak auditability.

### Accessibility and comprehensibility

A capability is not complete when only its authors can operate it. Products should work across relevant abilities, languages, devices, and levels of technical knowledge. Human-facing explanations should be actionable rather than ceremonial.

### Open-source reciprocity

Respect licenses, attribution, upstream communities, and maintainer capacity. Contribute where practical, fork honestly, and do not disguise local divergence as upstream obligation.

### Sustainable focus

Finite attention is an ethical constraint. Stop weak work, retire unsupported services, and avoid imposing maintenance burdens without owners or resources. Sustainable revenue is welcome when aligned with public benefit and user autonomy.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Softwareco will not knowingly:

1. deploy covert manipulation, dark patterns, deceptive anthropomorphism, or shame-based engagement;
2. sell or exploit private behavioral data as the default economic model;
3. let a steward, agent, model, prompt, or user interface silently acquire authority it does not hold;
4. encode consent, delegation, guardianship, privacy, or high-impact approval solely in prompt text when runtime enforcement is required;
5. claim production readiness, safety, fairness, or reliability without owner-level evidence;
6. expose secrets, private artifacts, or sensitive user information through public repositories, logs, telemetry, or model prompts;
7. automate irreversible or high-blast-radius actions without explicit authorization and recovery/compensation planning;
8. use accessibility, education, care, or household contexts as experimentation grounds without proportionate safeguards;
9. obscure failures, incidents, conflicts of interest, or evidence gaps to preserve momentum;
10. create coercive lock-in when a reasonable export, migration, or retirement path can be provided.

## AI-agent ethics

Harnessed agents must operate under explicit scope and tool boundaries. Their outputs are proposals or evidence until the owning surface accepts them. Human accountability cannot be delegated to a model.

For agent-mediated material actions:

- identify the delegator and accountable owner;
- state what the agent may decide and what it must escalate;
- preserve provenance and evidence;
- disclose uncertainty and relevant limitations;
- provide override, abort, and recovery paths;
- do not infer consent from silence unless an accepted governance agreement explicitly permits it.

## Ethics escalation

Anyone—human or agent—may raise an ethics objection without retaliation.

1. Pause the affected non-emergency action when continued execution could increase harm.
2. Record the concern, affected people, evidence, uncertainty, and immediate containment in the owner surface.
3. Escalate to the accountable Product or Service Owner.
4. Escalate unresolved cross-domain, privacy, safety, or authority concerns to the Softwareco Org Owner when lawfully appointed; while vacant, escalate to the currently lawful higher-level human owner and the relevant AI Society owner.
5. Use an AK decision membrane for durable policy, authority, or high-impact product changes.
6. Preserve an appeal path and record the outcome and rationale.

Emergency containment may proceed before normal review when necessary to prevent harm; canonical records and retrospective review follow as soon as practical.
