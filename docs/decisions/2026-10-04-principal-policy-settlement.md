## Alternatives and tradeoffs

Route A is selected as preferred direction because it follows accepted D167 and
avoids qualification/maintenance of two new-birth routes. Route B remains a dormant,
exact owner-admitted transitional alternative with its complete contract; this ADR
does not authorize it. No numerical speed advantage is claimed.

Rejected: permanent advisory-only posture after an admitted standing appointment;
persona-issued power; blanket D68 supersession; requiring whole-fleet retirement
before one qualified birth; downstream edits that hide source defects.

Initiative improves useful flow but demands impact classification and current owner
admission. Conservative parent-right holds reduce convenient retries but prevent
unauthorized renewal. Conditional acceptance permits planning now without pretending
that missing boundary mechanisms are implemented. No implementation language is
selected; TypeScript/pi-durable versus Elixir/raw OTP remains an empirical choice
under identical obligations, not an authority granted by this ADR.

decision prerequisites for properly admitted work are satisfied, not that owner
